# P8 — React client

> Read `ARCHITECTURE.md` at the repo root first. Follow `.claude/PACKET_RULES.md`.

**You own:** `client-portal/**`. Node v24.19.0 is already installed.
**Wave:** 4, after P6 has merged.

---

Build the full loop, because the product is only legible visually:

signup → country dropdown → city dropdown, or **"my city isn't listed"** → pre-filled manual form →
income and lifestyle tier → considered share of the balance → dashboard → add a goal → watch the
recompute and the tradeoffs → add a competing higher-priority goal → watch the re-ranking.

**Render provenance everywhere a number came from data**: the confidence badge (`OFFICIAL`,
`CONTRIBUTED`, `CROWDSOURCED`, `ESTIMATED`, `USER_PROVIDED`), the `as_of` date, and the staleness
banner when a figure has drifted. The user should always know whether they are reading a government
statistic or an estimate — that honesty is the product's main claim on trust, and hiding it would
make every other number look equally authoritative.

The "my city isn't listed" form must arrive **pre-filled** from the country estimate. Eleven blank
boxes is where users abandon signup.

**Done when:** a 90-second screen recording exists showing the recompute-with-tradeoffs loop.
